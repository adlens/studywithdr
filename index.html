About Me

Hi, I'm Dr Xin. I'm a private Maths & Science tutor with nearly seven years of experience, teaching from 11+ and GCSE through to A-Level and university.

My background is in Materials Science & Engineering, and I completed my PhD at the University of Manchester. As well as GCSE and A-Level teaching, I regularly teach university-level mathematics, including calculus, linear algebra, probability and statistics, mechanics, optimisation and mathematical modelling.

Over the years I've realised that many students don't lack ability or interest. Often they simply haven't been shown mathematics in a way that feels logical, intuitive and approachable. My goal isn't only to improve grades, but to help students build confidence, feel less anxious about problem solving, and think through problems step by step.

How I Teach

1

Find the real gap

Usually not "difficulty with maths" — a specific gap in reasoning, algebraic fluency, or exam technique that's built up over time.

2

Explain thinking aloud

Breaking problems down step by step and talking through the reasoning surfaces gaps early, so confidence builds as we go.

3

Structured support

Notes, worksheets, homework and feedback between every lesson, tailored to each student's level and learning style.

4

Calm, not stressful

Mathematics is a logical language. With the right approach, it can be understood step by step.
